# PaperSkillWork Repository Rules

1. Every paper lives under `papers/<paper-id>/`.
2. Complete an evidence audit before major Enhanced implementation.
3. Paper facts come from the paper and evidence audit.
4. Teaching narrative comes from `design/storyboard.md`.
5. Interactions come from `design/interaction-plan.md`.
6. Canonical and Enhanced are separate artifacts.
7. Do not modify canonical after freeze.
8. PaperSkill is a separate release repository; use its official import flow.
9. `paper.py` handles mechanics; Skills handle AI reasoning.
10. Never advance a workflow gate without explicit verification.
