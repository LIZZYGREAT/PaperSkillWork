# Evidence Verdict

## Purpose
Connect a claim to its protocol, observation, interpretation, and boundary.

## Use When
Readers need to decide whether a result supports a particular claim.

## Do Not Use When
The evidence is missing or the claim is too broad to judge responsibly.

## Minimum Interaction
Let readers inspect conditions first, then choose supported, too strong, or unsupported.

## Recommended Components
`EvidenceViewer`, `BenchmarkExplorer`, and `TermRef` for unfamiliar metrics.

## Teaching Goal
Teach claim calibration and source traceability.

## Anti-patterns
Do not reveal a verdict before showing the protocol and claim boundary.

## Example from Existing Paper
LwF's evidence audit separates reported outcomes from broader interpretations of retained behavior.
