# Progressive Validation

## Purpose
Show what evidence each validation tier can and cannot establish.

## Use When
The paper evaluates a method from controlled synthetic settings through increasingly realistic settings.

## Do Not Use When
There are no distinct validation tiers or the sequence is not supported by the source.

## Minimum Interaction
Select a tier and read its factors, established claims, and remaining limits.

## Recommended Components
Future `ProgressiveValidation` P2 pattern; use a static `BenchmarkExplorer` meanwhile.

## Teaching Goal
Keep claims proportional to the validation setting.

## Anti-patterns
Do not imply that success at an early tier guarantees success in a later environment.

## Example from Existing Paper
Use only where a paper explicitly compares synthetic and real or single-task and sequential settings.
