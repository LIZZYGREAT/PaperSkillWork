# Raw to Structured Representation

## Purpose
Explain how an input becomes an intermediate representation and then a structured output.

## Use When
The representational change itself is part of the paper's mechanism.

## Do Not Use When
The content is merely a chronological process with no meaningful representation shift.

## Minimum Interaction
Select one representation stage and compare its input, output, and changed meaning.

## Recommended Components
`RepresentationTransform`, `MultiViewInspector`, or `ArchitectureExplorer`.

## Teaching Goal
Make clear what information is retained, transformed, or discarded.

## Anti-patterns
Do not imply that an illustrative toy is measured paper data.

## Example from Existing Paper
The shared feature in LwF can be contrasted with the task-specific logits it feeds.
