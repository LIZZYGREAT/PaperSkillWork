The local PDF and the arXiv record establish the source, but do not establish a third-party reuse grant: the arXiv record for v2 links to its non-exclusive distribution license, which grants arXiv.org a license to distribute; the CVPR 2017 open-access repository says copyright remains with the authors or other copyright holders. No separate permission or open reuse license for these figures has been located. See [arXiv v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.07725), [arXiv non-exclusive distribution license](https://arxiv.org/licenses/nonexclusive-distrib/1.0/license.html), and [CVPR 2017 repository terms](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/CVPR2017).

The Page 9 plan proposes embedding Figures 2–4 as `PaperFigure` assets. Those three figures are therefore retained as important source references, but are not approved tutorial assets at this stage. The W4 learning spine can cite their findings and point readers to the paper; it must not embed or crop their images. If later work obtains permission, update the relevant asset decisions with the permission record and source files before selecting them for public use.

Its exact reported values are separately registered in `research/evidence-registry.yaml` and may support a newly authored, attributed data plot; such a plot must use only those table values and must not imply that Figure 2 or Figure 4 curve data were recovered. New teaching diagrams should express the method in original layouts and wording, not trace or reproduce the source artwork. The original PDF remains in `source/paper.pdf` for internal verification and is excluded from tutorial export.
